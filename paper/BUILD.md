# Manuscript build

The tested build sequence is:

```text
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
```

`Fig1_tikz.tex` and `Fig2_tikz.tex` are editable TikZ sources. Their compiled
PDFs are used by `main.tex`. Figures 3 and 4 are regenerated by
`../scripts/generate_figures.py` from the explicitly unverified manuscript
snapshots. The supplied `main.pdf` has 23 A4 pages.
